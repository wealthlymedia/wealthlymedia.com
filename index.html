The studio

A company that spent years getting the work right deserves video that shows it.

Wealthly Media is a small production studio in Richmond Hill. We film at your showroom, your site, your dining room or your floor, with the people who actually do the work, and we cut everything ourselves.

Nothing is outsourced and nothing is borrowed. Every second you see came out of your own place, which is the only reason anyone believes it.

  • No stock footage, ever.
  • No actors playing your customers.
  • No promises about results.
